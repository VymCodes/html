What is cubing?

Cubing is the activity of solving a Rubik’s Cube and other twisty puzzles as quickly and efficiently as possible. Many people practice different solving methods and algorithms to improve their speed and compete in official competitions. Cubing also helps develop problem-solving skills, memory, and hand-eye coordination while being a fun hobby for all ages.

In cubing competitions, participants race to solve puzzles like the Rubik’s Cube in the fastest time possible. Competitions can include prizes, rankings, and different categories for various puzzles and age groups.
